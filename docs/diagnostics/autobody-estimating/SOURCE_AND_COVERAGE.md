# Autobody Source and Coverage Register

## Implemented generic manual release

Release `tms-collision-navigation-2026.10.29-v1` contains 84 components and 249 explicitly enumerated operations across all 15 blueprint families. All 249 operations bind to component views. The operation-family counts are exposed by `coverageManifest()` rather than maintained as a separate hard-coded catalog.

The source is original Time Motor Sales generic navigation and estimator-entered scope. The drawings illustrate component relationships, not exact vehicle construction. No published commercial labor allowances, OEM procedures, exact-vehicle configuration approval, licensed vendor entitlement or qualified technical reviewer approval is claimed.

## External content dependencies

Exact pilot configurations and qualified reviewers have not been supplied. No provider credential, redisplay agreement or usable collision-content adapter has been established by this implementation. Do not label these missing inputs as completed or purchase subscriptions automatically.

Provider-based content requires a source register entry identifying supplier, supported API/access method, storage/redisplay permissions, source version, effective period, vehicle coverage and actual reviewer decision. Until then, generic/manual estimating is the supported mode and technical references are entered by authorized staff against their real source.

Structural, restraint, calibration and high-voltage components offer assessment or vendor paths rather than invented repair/sectioning instructions. Recorded manual references do not certify an OEM method or staff qualification.

## Validation boundary

Local database migration verification uses a recoverable backup, an actual restore into a newly created loopback verification database and before/after checksums of existing business tables. Synthetic financial/approval tests run only in that disposable database. Its fixtures are not real customer authorization or actual repair evidence.

Generic coverage and software verification are distinct from exact-vehicle content acceptance. Owner review/adoption and qualified technical content review remain separate decisions.
