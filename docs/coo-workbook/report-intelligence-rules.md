# COO Workbook Report Intelligence Rules

Status: supplemental source of truth

Purpose: define how the workbook turns daily execution into daily, weekly, and monthly reports without making staff recreate their day manually.

## Report Principle

Reports should be generated from source-backed work, not from memory.

The report intelligence layer should answer:

- What was planned?
- What was completed?
- What changed unexpectedly?
- What is blocked?
- What needs follow-up?
- What should carry over?
- What needs management review?
- What pattern is forming?

## Source-Backed Rule

Every report item should preserve:

- source type
- source id
- source link when available
- title
- detail
- tone or risk level
- review status

This makes drafts auditable and avoids vague report language.

## Seed Promotion Rules

Promote activity into Report Seeds when it is meaningful:

- completed Daily Focus item
- unresolved must-close Daily Focus item
- Daily Focus item with watch/blocked risk
- checklist item with update note
- yellow/red checklist item
- checklist item marked done when it represents meaningful work
- Quick Update
- Quick Update with follow-up
- support request/action
- carryover
- operating exception

Avoid seed noise:

- purely routine green checklist items with no update
- duplicate signals already represented by stronger source
- empty or vague updates
- items with no operational meaning

## Draft Section Rules

Daily reports should include sections such as:

- Completed / Closed Today
- Blockers and Risks
- Quick Updates / Unplanned Events
- Approval or Management Review
- Carryover for Tomorrow
- Daily Effectiveness / Coaching

Weekly reports should emphasize:

- repeated blockers
- accomplishments
- carryover patterns
- support/coaching themes
- customer promise risk
- operational bottlenecks

Monthly reports should emphasize:

- operating health
- trend changes
- repeated workflow constraints
- leadership decisions needed
- staff growth and training needs

## Review Status Rules

- Excluded seeds do not appear in generated drafts.
- Included seeds appear before candidates.
- Carried-over seeds appear in carryover/tomorrow sections.
- Candidate seeds may appear when meaningful, but should remain distinguishable from reviewed items.

## Tone Rules

- Green: completed or positive progress.
- Blue: neutral context or informational update.
- Yellow: risk, watch, delay, pending follow-up, or carryover.
- Red: blocked, urgent, approval-sensitive, or management review needed.

## Habit-Building Rule

Report generation should reward strong daily habits:

- planning early
- updating work while it is happening
- capturing interruptions
- raising blockers
- closing loops
- preparing tomorrow

The report should be the natural byproduct of a well-used workbook.
