# Purchase / Appraisal Capture Review

## Purpose

Purchase / Appraisal Capture gives staff one simple phone-friendly path to collect evidence before a purchase, trade-in appraisal, customer appraisal, auction bid, outside purchase, or wholesale review.

## Review Guardrails

- This workflow is review-only until approved for adoption.
- It must not overwrite or delete stored customer, inventory, estimate, quote, invoice, repair order, appraisal, or accounting records.
- Captured photos and documents are treated as evidence for review, not permanent record storage, until the approved backend storage policy is implemented.
- TAI may summarize readiness and risk, but final buy, pass, trade, wholesale, or appraisal decisions require manager approval.

## Evidence Packet

The packet should capture:

- Vehicle identity: VIN, plate, stock tag, keys, source reference.
- Mileage and condition: odometer, dash lights, exterior, interior, tires, damage, startup proof.
- Documents: title, registration, auction sheet, payoff, disclosure, bill of sale, trade worksheet.
- Price basis: bid, asking price, trade number, appraisal target, transport, recon reserve, comparable source.
- Decision context: buy, hold, pass, manager review, and missing proof.

## TAI Review Logic

TAI reviews the packet for:

- Identity confidence.
- Mileage and visible condition risk.
- Seller/source reliability.
- Title, lien, disclosure, and ownership risk.
- Market fit, bid ceiling, expected margin, recon reserve, and missing proof.

The output is a recommendation packet only: ready for review, missing evidence, suggested next action, and decision support. It does not create or modify official inventory records by itself.
