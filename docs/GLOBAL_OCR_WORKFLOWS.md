# Reviewed document reading across the application

This branch extends the existing local OCR engine; it does not add an external AI upload provider. All reading results are proposals. The existing domain writers retain authority over customer creation, inventory changes, expenses, stock receipts, permissions, approved amounts and payments.

Spending links the retained original to the expense on its existing save action, without storing another copy. Claim authorization and sales flows continue to attach through their existing verified upload operations; these may store a workflow-specific copy. Other workflows retain their existing evidence and approval controls.

## Limits and completeness

- Sales-document camera assembly, PDF upload validation and structured sales OCR support **1–28 pages per document**, retaining the 25 MB limit. The generic camera scanner keeps its default 12-page limit unless a workflow explicitly requests another supported limit.
- Spending receipts retain the existing PDF/JPEG/PNG and 10 MB attachment policy, with up to 10 pages for reading. Vendor packets retain 24 pages. Other document-reading profiles allow up to 28 pages.
- PDF reading splits copies into individual pages for extraction, leaving the original bytes unchanged. It rejects oversized packets before OCR starts and reports how many pages yielded text. It never silently treats an unread final page as complete.
- Generic document classification now recognizes receipts, purchase paperwork, vendor invoices, parts documents, title, transport, warranty, statements and sales/claim-authorization paperwork in addition to the original ID/business-card/estimate categories.
- Generic persisted ID intake masks license numbers to the last four and omits raw ID OCR text. Existing stored records are not rewritten. Classification scores remain parsing heuristics and are labeled as such.

## Review and data protection

The authenticated, same-origin reading endpoints check workflow access and require manager access for statements. Saved reads, page transcripts and original downloads are scoped to their creator and current organization; background processing rechecks active account, company grants and workflow permissions. File size, page limits and signatures are validated before retention. Responses are not cached, engine diagnostics are hidden, original bytes are checksum-verified on every read, and real paths must remain within private storage. Up to three queued/processing reads are allowed per staff member. The legacy synchronous endpoint remains available to existing clients with its original one-active-read-per-process guard. Expense duplicate queries follow employee/manager and organization scope; retained-receipt attachment also prevents reusing the same source across current expenses.

The shared panel shows original-file preview, source pages, editable fields/rows, conflicting candidates and reconciliation warnings. Editing invalidates confirmation. Existing non-empty form values are preserved unless replacement is confirmed. CSV exports quote values and protect against spreadsheet formula injection. Payment, consent, signer authority, title readiness, actual delivery, stock condition and canonical identity are never inferred as completed actions.

These are bounded, label-based document adapters using the local extraction engine, not a promise to understand every supplier layout, handwriting, damaged image or bank format. Unknown/conflicting information stays blank or requires correction. Existing inventory import history, vendor batch import and sales phone handoff keep their dedicated processing paths. This reliability pass applies to the shared `DocumentReadingPanel`, including its embedded spending, contact, parts, sales and administrative workflows; it does not replace the dedicated ID scanner or vendor batch importer.

## Saved reading workflow — 2026-11-07

Staff continue to **Scan → Review → Save**. Select a file and choose **Read document**. The response returns after private retention, with page progress visible while reading continues. **Continue a saved read** lists the most recent 20 reads for the current purpose and signed-in staff member. Reuploading identical bytes for the same purpose reopens the existing read and corrections. The same creator's original may support multiple purposes without a second retained original.

Completed pages commit separately. Processing leases and fencing tokens prevent stale workers overwriting progress. A failed read exposes **Retry remaining pages**, retaining completed pages. Unreadable pages stop processing for retry rather than silently treating a partial packet as complete; a clearer replacement scan may be necessary. Original uploads, page completion, retries, review revisions and source access are audited. Review revisions use optimistic concurrency: a second window receives a conflict, keeps its visible entries and must reopen to compare. Edits autosave after a short pause; wait for **Review saved** or select **Save review for later** before leaving. Applying/exporting waits for a successful save. Changes invalidate staff confirmation. Confirmation validates receipt reconciliation, parts arithmetic and statement dates/amounts.

**View source** shows the extracted page transcript with a unique exact excerpt highlighted and a link to that original PDF page. This is transcription highlighting, not a bounding box drawn on the image. Repeated/ambiguous excerpts are not arbitrarily highlighted. Original and reviewed values remain distinguishable. Original downloads recheck active staff access; CSV exports contain the reviewed data already loaded in the staff member's session.

Deployment requires migration `20261107200000_resumable_document_reading`, a regenerated Prisma client and configured private local document storage (`DOCUMENT_LIBRARY_LOCAL_STORAGE_ROOT`). A cloud-provider name alone is insufficient: this reader fails closed until a supported private byte-read adapter is connected. Original bytes and page/review data are restricted source records; retention/disposition continues through organizational document policy, with no automatic deletion introduced here.

Run `npm run documents:worker` as a supervised process using the same database, private storage root and OCR configuration as the web app. It resumes queued or expired jobs even when browsers are closed, and rechecks current staff access. `-- --once` drains one bounded queue pass for an external scheduler. The web routes also use Next `after()` for a bounded reading pass; reopening a queued read can resume it. For automatic completion after process restarts or across long packets without reopening, the worker must be running. This change does not deploy or register a production worker.

The release configuration now includes a private `document-reader` Compose service using the tooling image, the app's document volume and database, an unprivileged user, bounded logs and automatic restart. It exposes no port and starts after the migrated app is healthy. The production workflow checks the worker's queue/schema connection and read/write access to retained originals before completing deployment. Reader request validation follows the actual browser Host through the HTTPS proxy while retaining foreign-origin, malformed-origin and cross-site rejection.

## Table reading, matching and duplicate review — next refinement

The shared review now shows a compact field/row count and **Matches and duplicate checks**. Likely duplicates expand that section automatically; ordinary suggestions remain collapsed. Checks refresh from the staff member's current field corrections, without changing the reviewed entries. Suggestions link to existing records in another tab so the current review stays open. A saved-read link opens the appropriate purpose and saved review directly, subject to its existing creator/company access checks.

- Parts tables recognize printed headers for part number, description, quantity, unit cost and amount, including reordered columns and bounded wrapped descriptions. The existing conventional row format remains supported. Source text and page references remain attached to every extracted row. Incorrect printed arithmetic stays visible and editable; the review identifies the affected row and requires correction before confirmation/application. Incomplete or ambiguous wraps are reported, never joined across pages. Unsupported printed layouts are reported for manual entry instead of guessing a column assignment.
- Statements recognize separate debit/credit columns, either order, and an optional running-balance column. Balance values never become transaction amounts. Explicit empty pipe/tab cells or printed dashes can represent an unused debit/credit cell; both sides populated or otherwise unclear rows require source review. Repeated same-value printed transactions are retained separately. Existing dated amount/CR/DR rows remain supported when no conflicting printed table layout is present. Handwriting, arbitrary column sets and arbitrary bank formats remain outside these adapters.
- Vendor suggestions use normalized business names (punctuation, spacing, and `&` / `and`) within the same organization, only for active/preferred, nonarchived vendors and staff who can view the vendor registry. Multiple matching vendors remain separate suggestions. In spending intake, a unique normalized match can prepare the existing vendor selection only after staff confirmation; selecting an existing vendor never creates one or changes banking details.
- Vehicle suggestions use the existing discovery capability checks, followed by an exact full VIN or exact stock-number check inside those authorized search results and the current organization. A supplied VIN never silently falls back to stock or year/make/model. Stock disagreement is explicit. These are record suggestions, not canonical identity confirmation or automatic vehicle linking. The destination's existing vehicle and purchase-order/parts controls remain authoritative.
- Possible duplicate scans are detected by normalized business plus invoice/reference and a matching date or amount, or by business/date/amount together. These are warnings, not proof: different documents may legitimately share those values. The reader compares the creator's most recent 200 completed reads for the same purpose and different file checksum, preferring saved staff corrections. Spending also checks up to 100 current, nonsuperseded expenses on the reviewed date within the employee/manager organization scope. Matching candidate searches are bounded (up to 100 vendor-name candidates and 20 authorized vehicle search candidates); they are assistance, not an exhaustive duplicate audit. Exact-byte deduplication and existing spending-receipt reuse prevention remain in force. Possible duplicates do not merge, delete, post or approve anything.

The checks endpoint is authenticated through the scoped reading service, same-origin, payload-bounded and non-cached. Permission failures never trigger an unrestricted lookup. Unavailable checks return a review note instead of exposing engine diagnostics or silently claiming a successful check. No additional migration, provider connection, payment or legal-form change is introduced. Existing saved reads retain their stored suggestions, corrections and source history; expanded extraction applies to newly completed jobs. Restart the local reading worker when updating extraction code. Dedicated vendor-batch import and ID/barcode capture keep their separate processing paths.

Desktop 1440 px and phone 390 px browser checks passed for duplicate refresh, saved reviews, parts corrections, source highlighting and page width, with no accessibility findings.
