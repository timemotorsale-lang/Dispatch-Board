# Labor-guide starting estimates and measured job records

## Current coverage

Every one of the 879 preview operations now has a proposed labor allowance and a
scenario range. None is a measured technician-hour standard. There are zero
seeded actual-job observations and no licensed labor feed. Numbers generated by
the planning model are original AI-authored assumptions for review, not values
extracted or reverse engineered from an OEM procedure or stopwatch study.

Existing numeric demo allowances are retained with a DEMO_SEED basis. Other
operations use WORK_BREAKDOWN. Both remain UNVALIDATED. Production services and
the existing Labor Guide are unchanged.

## Reproducible calculation

`labor-guide.ts` defines an explicit baseline for each of the 81 groups and
operation-specific overrides for tasks with clearly different scope. The generic
baseline fills remaining operations; it is not a vehicle-specific lookup.

Diagnostic allowances are for an initial assessment. The model splits a repair's
planning allowance into setup/access (30%), operation (40%), reassembly (20%) and
verification (the remainder). Diagnostic shares are 10%, 60%, 10% and remainder.
Minutes are allocated as whole minutes that sum to the proposed hours. These are
modeled shares, not observed task durations.

Repair scenario bounds are 65% and 160% of the point estimate; diagnostic bounds
are 50% and 200%. Bounds are rounded to a tenth of an hour. They are not empirical
percentiles, confidence intervals or evidence of accuracy. No generic multiplier
converts warranty hours into retail hours. Access, vehicle configuration, job scope,
position, quantity and shared work must be reviewed before quoting. Rust and broken
fasteners need separately scoped work; unattended waiting is not technician labor.

## Public reference research

- [Ford TSB 20-2315](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2020/MC-10180531-0001.pdf),
  September 14, 2020, page 3: operation 202315A allows 9.7 warranty hours for a
  package involving four VCT units and PCM programming on eligible F-150 3.5L
  EcoBoost vehicles. Build date and Raptor status matter. This cannot be assigned
  to a generic single-phaser operation.
- [Ford TSB 20-2034](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2020/MC-10171861-0001.pdf),
  February 4, 2020, page 2: a heater-core repair package varies by layout and engine;
  the 3.5L EcoBoost cases span 7.7–8.5 warranty hours. It includes testing and cooling
  work, and requires specific build-date, block-heater and symptom conditions.
- [MOTOR Estimated Work Times](https://www.motor.com/products-services/data-products/estimated-work-times/)
  distinguishes estimated repair times from available warranty times.

These historical bulletin references appear in the relevant preview groups. They
are not imported as universal times, and current applicability or supersession is
not established. Their published numbers do not validate the planning model.

## Building the shop's actual evidence

Within an operation, expand Labor time, then Record a completed job. Capture the
job reference, exact year/make/model/engine/drivetrain/body-access configuration,
completion date and sum of hands-on technician hours. Confirm matching operation,
position and scope. Exclude waiting and unrelated work. Reports are manually
entered, not independently verified clock records.

Only matching operation IDs and normalized, identical configuration descriptions
are grouped. Duplicate job references within that group are rejected. The preview
shows count, median and observed range. Three distinct reports enable a manual
comparison; this product threshold is not statistical validation. Outliers remain
visible in the range and are not silently removed. Applying a reported median is
an explicit estimate action and retains configuration and sample count. It does
not approve or publish a standard. Editing hours marks them as manually entered.

Reports are versioned, validated, scoped to the signed-in reviewer's browser key
and stored separately from estimate drafts. Saving a draft does not overwrite job
records. Export reported times as JSON before clearing browser data. Other users,
browsers and devices do not share these preview records.

Production adoption needs durable authorized storage, technician clock events,
completed-job review, included-operation/overlap normalization and a documented
calibration and revision process. An advisor should see proposed hours, source and
scope; detailed evidence stays behind progressive disclosure.
