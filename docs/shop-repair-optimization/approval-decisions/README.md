## Purpose

This workspace records real, attributable Shop/Recon approval decisions against the prepared execution package. It does not treat planning text, recommended dispositions, prior Inventory authority, branch creation or document commits as Shop/Recon approval.

1. Complete [`AE-0 Authority and Session Record`](AE_00_AUTHORITY_AND_SESSION_RECORD.md).
2. Resolve the [`Authority Confirmation Packet`](AUTHORITY_CONFIRMATION_PACKET.md).
3. Select `ENTER_APPROVAL_EXECUTION` or `ENTER_WITH_SAFE_DEFERRALS`.
4. Process every decision in the [`Eight-Workshop Decision Gate`](EIGHT_WORKSHOP_DECISION_EXECUTION_GATE.md).
   - Completed: [`Workshop 1 Decision Session`](WORKSHOP_01_DECISION_SESSION.md) and [`Workshop 1 Conditions`](WORKSHOP_01_MISSING_INPUTS.md).
   - Completed: [`Workshop 2 Decision Session`](WORKSHOP_02_DECISION_SESSION.md).
   - Completed: [`Workshop 3 Decision Session`](WORKSHOP_03_DECISION_SESSION.md).
   - Completed: [`Workshop 4 Decision Session`](WORKSHOP_04_DECISION_SESSION.md).
   - Completed: [`Workshop 5 Decision Session`](WORKSHOP_05_DECISION_SESSION.md).
   - Completed: [`Workshop 6 Decision Session`](WORKSHOP_06_DECISION_SESSION.md).
   - Completed: [`Workshop 7 Decision Session`](WORKSHOP_07_DECISION_SESSION.md).
   - Current packet: [`Workshop 8 Decision Session`](WORKSHOP_08_DECISION_SESSION.md).
5. Record valid outcomes in the governing [`Decision Signature Journal`](../approval-execution/DECISION_SIGNATURE_JOURNAL.md).
6. Resolve or safely contain the [`Missing Inputs Register`](../approval-execution/MISSING_INPUTS_REGISTER.md).
7. Execute and accept the [`Tabletop Suite`](../approval-execution/TABLETOP_AND_ACCEPTANCE.md).
8. Complete the separate [`Master Authorization Resolution`](../approval-execution/MASTER_AUTHORIZATION_RESOLUTION.md).

## Decision integrity

Only an authorized human can close a decision. A valid record requires exact policy, approver and represented authority, scope/version/effective time, conditions, dependencies, conflicts/recusals, additional required signers, safe default, review/expiry and evidence. Eli cannot perform both sides of a genuinely independent review merely because he is an ultimate backup.
