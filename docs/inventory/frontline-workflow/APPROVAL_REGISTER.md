## 1. Decision states

- `OPEN` — dealership decision required.
- `PROPOSED` — documented recommendation awaiting approval.
- `APPROVED` — named authority approved exact scope/version/effective date.
- `REJECTED` — proposal rejected with rationale.
- `DEFERRED` — intentionally excluded from current slice.
- `SUPERSEDED` — replaced by a later approved decision.

Approval records must include decision owner, approver, date, effective scope, rationale, evidence, affected identifiers, and review/expiry when applicable. Chat agreement alone is not treated as production policy publication.

## 18. Documentation-to-code gate

The Acquisition and Transportation vertical slice may begin code design only after `ITF-APR-001` through `ITF-APR-006`, `ITF-APR-010` through `ITF-APR-015`, `ITF-APR-020` through `ITF-APR-025`, and applicable financial, timing, evidence, integration, and pilot decisions are either:

- approved for the pilot scope; or
- explicitly deferred with a safe behavior that prevents the affected command from being presented or executed.

The Receipt and Custody vertical slice additionally requires `ITF-APR-080` through `ITF-APR-089` to be approved or explicitly deferred with safe prevention behavior.

The Inspection and Triage vertical slice additionally requires `ITF-APR-090` through `ITF-APR-099` to be approved or explicitly deferred with safe prevention behavior.

The Recon Planning and Approval vertical slice additionally requires `ITF-APR-100` through `ITF-APR-109` to be approved or explicitly deferred with safe prevention behavior.

The Parts and Repair Execution vertical slice additionally requires `ITF-APR-110` through `ITF-APR-119` to be approved or explicitly deferred with safe prevention behavior.

The Quality and Safety Verification vertical slice additionally requires `ITF-APR-120` through `ITF-APR-129` to be approved or explicitly deferred with safe prevention behavior.

The Title and Document Readiness vertical slice additionally requires `ITF-APR-130` through `ITF-APR-139` to be approved or explicitly deferred with safe prevention behavior. `ITF-APR-130` cannot be deferred for any live pilot jurisdiction because the workflow must not invent legal applicability.

The Merchandising and Pricing vertical slice additionally requires `ITF-APR-140` through `ITF-APR-149` to be approved or explicitly deferred with safe prevention behavior. Any deferred content, price, channel, or provider authority must remove the affected action from the live pilot rather than seed a permissive default.

The Frontline Release Review vertical slice additionally requires `ITF-APR-150` through `ITF-APR-159` to be approved or explicitly deferred with safe prevention behavior. Critical-gate definitions, release authority, atomic authorization/projection, and suspension/reinstatement controls cannot be replaced by the existing basic description/price/photo threshold in a live pilot.

Approval to implement does not equal approval to activate. Activation requires the acceptance and pilot gates in [`ACCEPTANCE_AND_PILOT_PLAN.md`](ACCEPTANCE_AND_PILOT_PLAN.md).
