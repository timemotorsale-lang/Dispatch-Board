# Slice 2 Staff Playbook — Receive, Secure, Verify, Handoff

**Audience:** Receiving, Inventory, lot/custody, managers and Inspection/Triage staff
**Status:** Proposed operating playbook; training/tabletop only until approved
**Rule:** Record what is physically true. Never check a box merely to advance the vehicle.

## Staff experience

Staff should enter Slice 2 from a single vehicle workbench action: **Receive vehicle**. The workbench presents one current question at a time, while keeping identity, location, custody owner, restrictions, age and open exceptions visible. It must offer **Something is wrong / I cannot verify** at every consequential step.

## Receiver checklist

### A. Observe and secure

- Confirm the vehicle is physically present; do not rely only on a text, ETA or carrier status.
- Record the actual location and time.
- Identify the delivering party and authorized receiver when available.
- If after hours, unattended, misplaced or unsecured, use the approved containment route and stop normal receipt.
- Do not move an unsafe or unknown vehicle. Internal driving is currently excluded.

### B. Verify identity and custody

- Compare the physical VIN to the expected vehicle.
- Record the odometer observation and unit, or why it cannot be obtained.
- Record who transferred custody, who accepted/contained it, when and where.
- Stop on VIN mismatch, unreadable identity, disputed custody or suspected duplicate.

### C. Inventory what arrived

- Count and identify keys/access devices.
- Inventory physical documents without declaring title readiness.
- Compare promised/expected accessories and equipment.
- Do not place personal or sensitive details in general notes.
- If hazardous, prohibited or suspicious property is present, stop and use the approved restricted response.

### D. Record condition and restrictions

- Capture the required condition views only when safe and feasible.
- Compare with accepted pre-arrival evidence when available.
- Record observations, not accusations or unsupported causes.
- Classify whether movement/access is allowed, restricted, requires qualified equipment, or is prohibited.

### E. Resolve or own discrepancies

For every discrepancy select one controlled outcome: resolved with evidence; owned noncritical carry-forward; correction; claim review; return/rejection review; or critical hold. Record the owner, action, due/review time, escalation and downstream restriction.

### F. Complete and hand off

- Review the receipt summary before completing.
- Confirm that the receipt gate result matches the evidence.
- Offer the complete handoff to a named Inspection/Triage owner or eligible queue.
- If Inspection cannot accept, keep the vehicle in safe custody with a visible capacity-wait owner and clock.

## Manager exception checklist

Before accepting an exception, verify:

- the physical facts and original evidence remain unchanged;
- the exception is noncritical and permitted by approved policy;
- the decision maker has the required authority and no prohibited conflict;
- the owner, action, review/expiry, escalation and restrictions are explicit;
- receipt and inspection states remain distinct; and
- correction, claim, return/rejection or risk acceptance did not occur implicitly.

## Inspection consumer checklist

Accept the handoff only when the receipt version, identity, custody, location, condition, property status, restrictions, exceptions and evidence references are present. Reject with a precise correctable reason when the envelope is incomplete. Use capacity wait—not rejection—when the envelope is valid but no eligible inspection resource is currently available.

## Stop-and-escalate conditions

- VIN mismatch, unreadable identity or duplicate concern;
- unknown/disputed custody or unsecured placement;
- unsafe movement, active leak, fire, EV/high-voltage, environmental or structural concern;
- personal/sensitive/hazardous/prohibited property without an approved response;
- mandatory evidence missing, rejected or unreliable;
- material unowned discrepancy;
- unauthorized command, stale/conflicting data or concurrent completion conflict;
- no eligible receiving, containment or inspection location.

The safe result is an attributable hold with physical custody preserved—not a completed receipt.
