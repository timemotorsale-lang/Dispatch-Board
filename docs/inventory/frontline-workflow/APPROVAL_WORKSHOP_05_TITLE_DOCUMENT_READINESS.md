## 6. Minimum tabletop cases

1. Complete ordinary title packet.
2. Wrong VIN/party/assignment/signature/date.
3. Odometer discrepancy or disclosure requirement.
4. Brand/history/disclosure conflict.
5. Missing ownership-chain link.
6. POA/entity signer capacity ambiguity.
7. Payoff sent but lien release absent.
8. Duplicate/lost-title special process.
9. Scan exists but required original custody unknown.
10. Original lost/damaged/misdirected or privacy exposure.
11. Obsolete form after packet preparation.
12. Provider acknowledgement versus agency completion.
13. Provider rejection/outage/resubmission.
14. Filing fee exceeds authority.
15. Constrained state with merchandising pressure.
16. Post-ready evidence expires/changes and triggers suspension.
