## Authorization boundaries

1. Policy approval does not authorize design, implementation, shadow operation, pilot or activation unless separately stated.
2. Implementation authorization applies only to the named slice, version, scope and conditions.
3. Pilot authorization applies only to the named implemented version, cohort, location, period and monitoring/rollback plan.
4. Activation requires separate evidence and authorization after pilot exit.
5. Automatic start and simultaneous activation of all nine slices are expressly prohibited.
6. No approval permits autonomous acquisition, spend, movement, repair scope, title/legal conclusion, customer price, publication, frontline release, reservation, sale or delivery.
