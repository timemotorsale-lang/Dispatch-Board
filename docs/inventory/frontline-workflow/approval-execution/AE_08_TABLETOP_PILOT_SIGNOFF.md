## Purpose

Prove that the approved or safely deferred policies work together across representative normal, exception, degraded, correction, reversal, safety, privacy, financial and recovery cases. This milestone does not repeat or silently replace individual decisions.

## Required participants

Authorized GM/Operations, Inventory, receiving/custody, Shop/Recon/Parts/QC/Safety, Office/Controller/Title, Merchandising/Pricing/Channel, Sales/Lot, Privacy/Security/Data/Integration, workflow owner, pilot owner, and representative staff. Qualified title/legal and safety authorities participate in their applicable cases.

## Required evidence

- Completed dispositions and exact effective policy versions.
- Tabletop record for the minimum enterprise suite.
- Staff role/usability confirmation and corrections.
- No unresolved uncontained critical gap.
- Slice-specific implementation, pilot and activation eligibility.
- Monitoring, incident, pause, rollback, resume and backup authorities.
- Manual/degraded runbook and reconciliation proof.

## Possible final results

- `NO_GO` — approval, control, evidence or critical scenario failure remains.
- `DESIGN_ONLY` — named slices may enter technical design; no code/pilot authorization.
- `IMPLEMENTATION_GO` — named slices may be implemented; no pilot/activation authorization.
- `SHADOW_GO` — named implemented version may evaluate without changing work.
- `MANAGER_CONFIRMED_PILOT_GO` — named version/cohort/time window may pilot.

Automatic activation always requires a separate later decision.
