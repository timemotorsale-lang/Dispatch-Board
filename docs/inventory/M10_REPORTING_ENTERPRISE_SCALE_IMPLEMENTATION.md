# M10 — Reporting and enterprise scale

M10 provides governed reporting and scale-evidence foundations for VEH-130–VEH-139.

Delivered controls include immutable daily metric facts, scorecards, bottleneck/cohort analytics, forecast backtesting with drift and uncertainty gates, advisory capacity and inventory pilots, evidence-backed responsible summaries, multi-location transfer reconciliation, tenant-isolated policy inheritance, and enterprise scale/recovery gates.

All recommendations remain advisory and manager-controlled. Forecasts and summaries require evidence, uncertainty, privacy, evaluation, fallback, and kill-switch controls. Scale gates record evidence; they do not claim that a production load test, regional failover, or provider recovery drill has already occurred.
