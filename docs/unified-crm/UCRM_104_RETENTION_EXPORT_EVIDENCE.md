# UCRM-104 Advanced Retention, Legal Hold, and Export Evidence

**Status:** `HELD`  
**Date:** 2026-08-27  
**Branch:** `codex/unified-crm-ucrm080-099`

UCRM-104 adds a safe readiness boundary for retention, legal hold, and export. It does not enable automated disposition, broad export, or restricted export.

This milestone remains held under `UCRM-DEC-014` until exact retention periods, disposition schedules, export thresholds, simulation proof, recovery, legal/security signoff, browser proof, and rollback evidence are approved.
