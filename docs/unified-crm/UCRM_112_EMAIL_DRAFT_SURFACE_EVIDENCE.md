# UCRM-112 Email Draft Surface Evidence

**Status:** `IMPLEMENTED_UNVERIFIED`  
**Date:** 2026-08-27  
**Branch:** `codex/unified-crm-ucrm080-099`

UCRM-112 adds a review-only email draft readiness surface. It does not send email, sync mailboxes, store provider credentials, or mark anything delivered.

The draft surface keeps provider send and mailbox sync held while making safe draft copy, consent reminders, fallback, and no-send language explicit.
