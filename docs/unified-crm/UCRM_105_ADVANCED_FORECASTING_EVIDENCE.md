# UCRM-105 Advanced Forecasting and Attribution Evidence

**Status:** `HELD`  
**Date:** 2026-08-27  
**Branch:** `codex/unified-crm-ucrm080-099`

UCRM-105 adds a safe readiness boundary for advanced forecasting and attribution. It does not enable predictive forecasts or attribution claims.

The existing deterministic forecast baseline remains the fallback until explainability, freshness, historical evaluation, override separation, bias/error review, Product Owner approval, browser proof, and rollback evidence are complete.
