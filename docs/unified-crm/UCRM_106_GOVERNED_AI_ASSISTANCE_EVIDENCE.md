# UCRM-106 Governed AI Assistance Evidence

**Status:** `HELD`  
**Date:** 2026-08-27  
**Branch:** `codex/unified-crm-ucrm080-099`

UCRM-106 adds a safe readiness boundary for governed AI assistance, including future AI receptionist support. It does not enable AI drafts, AI receptionist behavior, autonomous actions, or customer communications.

The boundary requires permission-aware retrieval, citations/provenance, evaluation sets, prompt-injection defense, human approval, cost/latency controls, logging, opt-out handling, and a kill switch before activation.
