# CRM Next — Product Blueprint

## Product identity

CRM Next is the Customer Command Center module inside the broader Time Motor Sales Dispatch Board. It is neither a separate application nor a cosmetic refresh of Legacy.

## Product promise

The CRM should answer quickly: **Who needs attention? Why? What should the employee do next? What happens after that action?**

## Design constitution

- Enterprise depth underneath; simple execution above.
- `ATTEND` is the primary execution concept.
- Interaction hierarchy: **Attend → Work → Investigate**.
- Customer 360 groups: **Overview / Sales / Ownership / Support / Activity**.
- Do not expose all customer domains as equal tabs.
- Customer Workbench is the primary execution workspace; desktop target is approximately 70/30 execution/context.
- One unified Customer Activity Timeline.
- Intelligence outputs remain distinct: **Risk / Next Action / Opportunity**.
- Recommendations must be explainable.
- AI begins with **Recommend → Explain → Human acts**.
- CRM work uses the shared Dispatch Board task engine.
- CRM and Dispatch Board open the same underlying task.
- Outcomes feed back into Customer 360 and its timeline.
- Progressive disclosure protects simplicity.

## Customer 360 capability scope

Leads; Communications; Appointments; Test Drives; Deals; Vehicles Purchased; BHPH/Financing; Payments; Service/Repairs; Rentals/Loaners; Documents/Titles; Customer Issues; Insurance Claims; Tasks; Complete Activity Timeline.

## Primary CRM areas

Command Center; Customers; Needs Attention; Pipeline; Appointments; Cases; Intelligence; Reports; CRM Settings.

## Closed loop

Customer 360 → signals/activity → Intelligence → Risk/Next Action/Opportunity → Priority → shared Task Engine → Dispatch Board/Employee → action/outcome → Activity Event → Customer 360.

## Boundaries

CRM Next is not a second Dispatch Board, second task database, full accounting ledger, automatic DMS replacement, 15-tab record, or unreviewable AI decision maker.
