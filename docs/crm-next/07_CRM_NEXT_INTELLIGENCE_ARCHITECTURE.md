# CRM Next — Intelligence & Priority Architecture

## Purpose

Turn Customer 360 signals into explainable attention and work, not opaque AI output.

## Risk

Potential negative outcome: unresolved complaint, overdue title, missed appointment, delinquency, stalled repair, insurance deadline, repeated contact without resolution.

## Next Action

Concrete action: call, text, verify submission, prepare vehicle, collect document, confirm appointment, request approval.

## Opportunity

Potential beneficial action: hot lead, re-engagement, repeat purchase, trade opportunity, stalled-deal recovery, referral.

## Pipeline

Signals → rules/models → IntelligenceSignal → Priority Engine → Needs Attention → human action/shared Task → outcome → feedback event.

## Priority factors

Urgency/deadline; customer impact; revenue opportunity; risk/severity; aging; commitment/SLA; engagement; blocked/dependency state; manager escalation.

## Explainability contract

Every surfaced recommendation includes what is recommended, why, evidence/signals, urgency, related record, available action, and ability to dismiss/snooze/mark not relevant when appropriate.

## Automation maturity

1. Deterministic rules and explainable recommendations.
2. Assisted AI summarization/classification with human confirmation.
3. Low-risk automated actions with audit trail and opt-out.

High-impact actions remain governed by explicit policy.

## Feedback

Capture accepted, completed, snoozed, dismissed, not relevant, reassigned, escalated. Feedback can improve prioritization without silently changing policy.
