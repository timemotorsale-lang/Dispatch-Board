# CRM Next — Acceptance & Promotion Criteria

## Customer 360

Customer can be found quickly; identity is reliable; Sales/Ownership/Support/Activity groups work; complete timeline is understandable; open items and current situation are obvious.

## Execution

Needs Attention works; Attend opens the correct Workbench; next action is visible/explainable; universal actions work; outcomes are captured; follow-up/task creation is low-friction.

## Enterprise capabilities

Leads; communications; appointments; test drives; deals; vehicle ownership; BHPH/financing context; payments context; service/repairs; rentals/loaners; documents/titles; customer issues; insurance claims; tasks; timeline are present or explicitly deferred by approved scope.

## Intelligence

Risk, Next Action, and Opportunity are distinct; prioritization is explainable; stale signals expire/resolve; users can provide feedback; manager can review/override as allowed.

## Shared platform

No duplicate task universe; appointments integrate with Appointment Command Center; permissions are server-enforced; activity is event-backed; customer work appears correctly on Dispatch Board.

## UX

Employee can determine what to do next without navigating many modules. Manager can identify failures to act. Desktop/tablet/mobile are usable. Loading/empty/error states are honest. Important actions remain visible.

## Migration

Legacy capability audit complete; data reconciliation passes; rollback tested; no critical capability missing; staff scenario testing passes; management explicitly approves promotion.
