# Development inventory review pack

`inventory.json` contains 24 fictional review records, not dealership stock, imported listings, market valuations or verified manufacturer specifications. All prices, mileage, trim descriptions, features and specifications are synthetic scenarios. Existing RAV4 baseline records remain for regression testing; their missing specifications deliberately remain unknown.

The pack covers sedans, SUVs, crossovers, trucks, hatchbacks, a wagon, convertible, coupe and vans; gasoline, diesel, hybrid, plug-in hybrid and electric; manual, automatic, CVT and single-speed transmissions; all standard exterior/interior color families; features, seats, cylinders and price drops. There are 23 visible unsold examples and one sold fixture for purchase-blocking review. Electric examples do not fabricate combined MPG from MPGe.

There are no customer records, contacts, VINs, ownership/history claims or real offers. The preview loader fills missing photos from the bundled, generated body-style image library (10 assets including the original RAV4). Each displayed card/detail photo is labeled illustrative; vehicles and colors may differ. This is not a make/model/trim/photo match or evidence of an actual unit. Production never receives these fallback images. Real inventory requires approved unit-specific media; no competitor photographs are imported.

Sedan, truck, coupe, electric crossover and minivan JPEGs are copies of the existing generated Design Studio assets; their prompts/provenance remain in `src/features/website-console/design-studio/assets/README.md`. Four additional built-in image-generation outputs were created for this review and JPEG-optimized to 960 pixels wide: a blue compact five-door hatchback, green family station wagon, gold two-seat open-roof convertible, and white tall-roof passenger van. Shared prompt: single photorealistic fictional vehicle, front three-quarter view, leafy Minneapolis park, autumn daylight, fully visible wheels/body, realistic reflections, wide 3:2 card composition, no text/manufacturer badges/readable plates/people/watermark. Images are representative body-style artwork, not a source of specifications or color facts. Generated originals remain in the local generator library; final project JPEGs live in `fixtures/media/`.

The existing sample policy loads these files only in explicitly enabled local development or authorized private review. Production does not read these fixtures as fallback; LIVE feeds reject sample identifiers/provenance/media. No production feed, database, provider, account or submission setting is changed. Refresh `/shop` on the local development site to review the pack. Restart the development server if a cached route remains visible.
